QuestDrop

Send your own videos to your Meta Quest — no cable fuss, no cloud.

QuestDrop moves video and other files that you choose from your Android phone or PC to your Meta Quest headset, directly over your home Wi-Fi or a USB cable. Files never go through the internet.

Features

  • Pair your phone or PC once, then send files with a tap.
  • Transfers keep running in the background, with progress and time left.
  • See, play and delete the videos already on your Quest.
  • Works with the video players you already use on Quest.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.

Important

QuestDrop does not provide, stream or download movies. It only moves files you already own between your own devices.
